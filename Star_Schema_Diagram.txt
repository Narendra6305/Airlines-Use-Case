TABLE DEFINITIONS:

================================================================================
DIM_FLIGHTS - Flight Dimension Table
================================================================================
column_name        | data_type    | description
-------------------|--------------|---------------------------
flight_id          | VARCHAR(50)  | Primary Key
airline            | VARCHAR(50)  | Airline name
source             | VARCHAR(10)  | Departure airport code
destination        | VARCHAR(10)  | Arrival airport code
route              | VARCHAR(30)  | Source -> Destination
departure_time     | TIMESTAMP    | Scheduled departure
arrival_time       | TIMESTAMP    | Scheduled arrival
duration_minutes   | DECIMAL(10,2)| Flight duration in mins
duration_bucket    | VARCHAR(20)  | <2H, 2-3H, >3H

================================================================================
DIM_PASSENGERS - Passenger Dimension Table
================================================================================
column_name        | data_type    | description
-------------------|--------------|---------------------------
passenger_id       | VARCHAR(50)  | Primary Key
first_name         | VARCHAR(50)  | First name
last_name          | VARCHAR(50)  | Last name
age                | INTEGER      | Age in years
gender             | VARCHAR(10)  | MALE/FEMALE
email              | VARCHAR(100) | MASKED email
phone              | VARCHAR(20)  | MASKED phone
aadhaar_id         | VARCHAR(64)  | SHA-256 hashed
date_of_birth      | DATE         | Date of birth

================================================================================
DIM_PAYMENTS - Payment Dimension Table
================================================================================
column_name        | data_type    | description
-------------------|--------------|---------------------------
payment_id         | VARCHAR(50)  | Primary Key
booking_id         | VARCHAR(50)  | Foreign Key to booking
amount             | DECIMAL(10,2)| Payment amount
payment_method     | VARCHAR(20)  | UPI, CARD, NETBANKING

================================================================================
FACT_BOOKINGS - Fact Table (Central Transaction Table)
================================================================================
column_name              | data_type    | description
-------------------------|--------------|---------------------------
booking_id               | VARCHAR(50)  | Primary Key
passenger_id             | VARCHAR(50)  | FK to passengers
flight_id                | VARCHAR(50)  | FK to flights
payment_id               | VARCHAR(50)  | FK to payments
booking_date             | DATE         | Booking date
status                   | VARCHAR(20)  | CONFIRMED/CANCELLED/PENDING
seat_number              | VARCHAR(10)  | Allocated seat
passport_number          | VARCHAR(64)  | SHA-256 hashed
emergency_contact_name   | VARCHAR(100) | Emergency contact
emergency_contact_phone  | VARCHAR(20)  | MASKED phone
